Support

We’re happy to help. Write to support@upmood.app and we’ll get back to you, usually within a few days. In the app, you can also go to Settings › Send feedback, which opens an email to us.

Common questions

I’m not getting notifications

Check iOS Settings › Notifications › Upmood is on, then open Upmood › Settings and check your check-in time. It can also mean there is nothing more to send today.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Upmood › Settings › Delete account. Your account and everything in it is removed. You can also email us and we’ll do it for you.

What do you do with my moods?

Only choose your quotes and build your history and weekly summary. Never ads, never sold. Read the Privacy Policy.

Where do the quotes come from? One looks wrong.

Quotes are collected with the help of AI (Google Gemini) and shown with the name of the person they are attributed to. AI can get a quote’s wording or its author wrong. If you spot one, email us with the quote and we will check it and correct or remove it.

If you are in crisis
Upmood is gentle encouragement, not medical care. If you are struggling or in danger, please contact a mental health professional or your local emergency number.
